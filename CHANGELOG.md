# FitRank Changelog

## 2026-09-24

- FitRank moved from a school interface prototype into a working app structure with Home, Workouts, Progress, Food, and Settings sections.
- Workout logging, ELO, ranks, history, and persistent local progress are working. Food tracking remains a placeholder.
- The interface atmosphere was refined with clearer typography, spacing, contrast, and subtle motion.
- The roadmap now includes a larger exercise library, logger improvements, Google Sign-In, secure cloud storage, Muscle Map, improved food logging, and PWA support. Account plans include privacy, security, and control over stored data.
